---
title: "Receiving Hours & Deliveries"
description: "Required receiving windows and delivery instructions for inventory sent to HexPrep."
---

Use the correct receiving window for every shipment sent to HexPrep.

<Warning>
  **Warehouse receiving hours are Monday–Friday, 10:00 AM–6:00 PM in the destination warehouse’s local time.** Oregon uses Pacific Time and Illinois uses Central Time. Do not schedule delivery before 10:00 AM or after 6:00 PM.
</Warning>

## Before any order ships

Provide the receiving window to every retailer, store, supplier, distributor, and carrier sending inventory to HexPrep.

Add the hours to the order or purchase-order delivery instructions whenever that field is available. Verify the sender has the correct window before the order is released or dispatched.

## Delivery attempts outside the receiving window

Delivery attempts outside the official receiving window may be refused, rescheduled, or returned by the carrier. Do not assume an early or after-hours route will leave the shipment.

<Info>
  A retailer, supplier, distributor, or carrier scan showing **“delivered”** is not proof that HexPrep accepted the shipment. Inventory is considered received only after HexPrep physically accepts it and the shipment is checked into the dashboard.
</Info>

## Pre-shipment checklist

Before inventory moves, confirm:

- The correct HexPrep warehouse was assigned.
- The sender has the warehouse’s local receiving window.
- The order or purchase-order delivery notes include the receiving hours.
- Your approved shipping name and client code are included where required.
- The inbound record is created in the HexPrep dashboard.
- Tracking is added when available.

If a sender cannot deliver during the official receiving window, contact HexPrep before dispatch. Do not assume an after-hours exception has been approved.
