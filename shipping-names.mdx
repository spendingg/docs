title: "Shipping Names"
---

As a client, you have the ability to add up to 3 shipping names for packages shipped to our warehouse.

<Frame>
  ![Shipping Names](/images/shipping-names.png)
</Frame>

### Key Benefits & Details

- **Improved Identification:** Associates different names or aliases with your shipments, making it easier for our warehouse to match incoming packages to your account.
- **Flexibility:** Particularly useful if you are ordering from multiple suppliers or <Tooltip tip="The practice of subtly altering personal information to bypass retailer restrictions that limit purchases to one per customer." headline="Meaning">jigging</Tooltip>.
- **Account Limits:** You are restricted to a maximum of 3 active shipping names at any given time to keep your records organized and prevent processing errors. To raise this limit request a name increase. 

### Best Practices for Your Shipping Names

- **Accuracy is Key:** Ensure that the shipping label on the package matches one of your 3 approved shipping names to avoid receiving delays or mismatches.
- **Unique code**: If you did not include your unique code in your last name, please ensure the code is included in at least one of your three shipping names.  

<Danger>
  Shipping inventory to our warehouse **without** the correct shipping names listed on our dashboard will result in significant delays. **HexPrep is not responsible for these delays**.
</Danger>
