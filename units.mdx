title: "Units"
---

The amount of inventory that is currently checked-in at our warehouse. You can filter out by fulfillment types and view inventory that is damaged by the retailer or carrier. 

<Frame>
  ![Units](/images/units-.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  You can request pictures or disposal **using** the 'Request Actions' button for damaged inventory. You can also select the **SKUs** you **want** outbounded and then click 'Shipment'.
</Tip>

# Request Outbound

You can instruct our team to begin shipping out your inventory. This is mainly for clients who have **the** 'On Client Request' option enabled, or for whenever you are ready for us to ship your items.

<Frame>
  ![Request Outbound](/images/request-outbound.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
  To avoid our storage fees, ensure you request an outbound before 14 days.
</Warning>
