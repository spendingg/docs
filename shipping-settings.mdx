---
title: "Shipping Settings"
---

You have the ability to choose your FBA shipping frequency. 

<Frame>
  ![Shipping Settings](/images/shipping-settings.png)
</Frame>

<Check>
  If you do not have time to manually request FBA shipments, do not select 'On client request'; instead, choose 'Minimum number of units' and set a threshold that is reasonable for your volume.
</Check>

<Warning>
  Setting an unreasonable amount for 'Minimum number of units' will lead to longer outbound times and **result in** storage fees after 14 days.
</Warning>
