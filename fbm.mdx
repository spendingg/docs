---
title: "Fbm"
---

Before inbounding your FBM inventory, ensure the SKU begins with **'PFL'**—the remaining characters are up to you. Failure to comply will result in delayed outbounds. This prefix allows us to filter out FBM orders not intended for our warehouse.

<Frame>
  ![Fbm](/images/fbm.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  This is **only** for Amazon FBM. You are not required to use 'PFL' for FBA SKU's. 
</Tip>
