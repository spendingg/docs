The 'Needs Attention' section is for inventory that requires review. This applies to inventory that was not inbounded by the client.

<Warning>
  Failure to correctly inbound inventory will **result in** significant delays. HexPrep **prioritizes** correctly inbounded shipments. Continued non-compliance after warnings may **incur an** 'Inbound Not Created' fee.
</Warning>
