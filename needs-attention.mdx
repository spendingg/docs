The 'Needs Attention' section is for inventory that requires review. This applies to inventory that was not inbounded by the client.

<Warning>
  Failure to correctly inbound inventory will **result in** significant delays. HexPrep **prioritizes** correctly inbounded shipments.
</Warning>

You’ll need to select the inventory and click the **"Convert to Inventory"** button.

<Frame>
  ![Selecting Inventory](/images/Selecting-Inventory.png)
</Frame>

Select the fulfillment method, then enter the ASIN and SKU if required.

<Frame>
  ![Entering Details](/images/Entering-Details.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Check the **"Needs Attention"** tab daily to ensure no inventory was missed during inbounding.
</Tip>
